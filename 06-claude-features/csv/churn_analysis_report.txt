====================================================================================================
STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
====================================================================================================

1. Engagement Intervention Program
  • Target customers with <60 hours viewing/month for engagement campaigns
  • Send personalized content recommendations to increase unique titles watched
  • Implement 'continue watching' reminders to boost session frequency

2. Customer Service Quality Improvement
  • High CS interactions strongly predict churn - focus on first-contact resolution
  • Proactively reach out to customers after 2+ CS interactions
  • Implement satisfaction surveys after each CS interaction

3. Subscription Tier Optimization
  • Review Basic tier value proposition (43.5% churn rate is critical)
  • Consider promotional upgrades for engaged Basic tier users
  • Premium tier retention is strong (24%) - analyze what works there

4. Content Strategy
  • Monitor customers with Horror/Thriller preferences (highest churn)
  • Expand content library in high-churn genres
  • Promote diverse content discovery to increase unique titles viewed

5. Early Warning System
  • Flag customers showing: <50 hrs viewing, <5 binge sessions, >3 CS contacts
  • Implement automated retention campaigns for at-risk segments
  • Monthly churn risk scoring based on engagement metrics

====================================================================================================
CONCLUSION
====================================================================================================

The analysis reveals that churn is primarily driven by LOW ENGAGEMENT rather than
demographics or content preferences. Churned customers consistently show:

  • 20% fewer viewing hours
  • 14% shorter average sessions
  • 18% fewer unique titles explored
  • 20% fewer binge-watching sessions
  • 28% MORE customer service interactions

These patterns suggest that addressing engagement and satisfaction issues early
can significantly reduce churn. The combination of usage monitoring and proactive
intervention represents the highest-impact retention strategy.
